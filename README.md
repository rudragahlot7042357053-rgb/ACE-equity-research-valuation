# ActionAction Construction Equipment Ltd. – Equity Research & Business Valuation

## Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive equity research and business valuation analysis of Action Construction Equipment Ltd. (ACE), covering its business profile, industry environment, historical financial performance, forecasting assumptions, financial ratios, and valuation.

The analysis applies Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) and relative peer valuation methods to assess the company's intrinsic value, financial strength, key risks, and investment attractiveness.
