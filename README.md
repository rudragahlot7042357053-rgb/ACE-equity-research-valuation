# ActionAction Construction Equipment Ltd. – Equity Research & Business Valuation

## Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive equity research and business valuation analysis of Action Construction Equipment Ltd. (ACE), covering its business profile, industry environment, historical financial performance, forecasting assumptions, financial ratios, and valuation.

The analysis applies Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) and relative peer valuation methods to assess the company's intrinsic value, financial strength, key risks, and investment attractiveness.
## Business Problem / Objective

The objective of this project was to evaluate the financial performance, business prospects, and investment attractiveness of Action Construction Equipment Ltd. by analysing its historical performance, forecasting future financials, and assessing key financial ratios.

The analysis aimed to estimate the company's intrinsic value using DCF and relative valuation methods and determine whether its prevailing market price offered an attractive risk–return opportunity for investors.
## Project Context

The analysis was undertaken in the context of India's infrastructure-led growth and the resulting demand for construction and material-handling equipment. Action Construction Equipment Ltd. operates in this industry with a strong position in cranes and an expanding product portfolio.

Given the company's strong historical financial growth, improving profitability and near-zero leverage, alongside a relatively high market valuation, the project focused on assessing whether the company's fundamentals and future growth prospects were adequately reflected in its prevailing market price.
## My Role / Contribution

Independently conducted the complete equity research process for Action Construction Equipment Ltd., from company and industry analysis to financial statement analysis, forecasting, ratio analysis, and valuation.

Developed the DCF valuation including WACC, FCFF, terminal value and sensitivity analysis, performed relative/peer valuation and risk assessment, interpreted the findings, and prepared the complete equity research report with the final investment recommendation.
## Approach

The analysis followed a structured equity research approach. It began with an assessment of ACE's business model, competitive position and industry environment, followed by analysis of historical income statements, balance sheets and cash flows.

Future financial performance was then forecast for FY26E–FY30E using company-specific and historical assumptions, followed by profitability, liquidity, leverage and valuation-ratio analysis.

The valuation stage included WACC estimation, FCFF-based DCF valuation, terminal value and sensitivity analysis, complemented by relative valuation against selected peers. The findings from the fundamental analysis and valuation were integrated with key business risks and potential catalysts to arrive at the final investment recommendation.

## Key Output

The project produced a complete equity research report supported by historical financial analysis, FY26E–FY30E financial forecasts, ratio analysis, WACC and FCFF-based DCF valuation, sensitivity analysis, and peer valuation.

The analysis estimated ACE's intrinsic value at ₹624.77 per share against a market price of ₹817 and concluded with a SELL/REDUCE recommendation based on the valuation gap, while identifying the key business risks and potential upside catalysts.

## Key Insight & Recommendation

The key insight was that ACE combines strong business fundamentals with a strong balance sheet and attractive long-term industry tailwinds, but its prevailing market valuation leaves limited margin of safety.

The DCF analysis estimated an intrinsic value of ₹624.77 per share versus the market price of ₹817, indicating approximately 23.5% downside. Based on the combined fundamental, valuation and risk assessment, the report recommended SELL/REDUCE at the prevailing price, while considering the ₹625–₹680 range more attractive for long-term investors.
